---
title: "Phase III study of tolugepant in chronic migraine prevention does not meet its primary endpoint"
date: "2026-03-12"
published: true
category: "Clinical"
summary: "The acute indication for Migrestil is not affected. The prevention programme is discontinued."
---

NeuroStat today reported that a Phase III study of tolugepant in the prevention of chronic migraine did not meet its primary endpoint, a reduction in monthly migraine days compared with placebo. No new safety signals were found. The programme is discontinued. Migrestil stays approved for the acute treatment of migraine, and the study does not affect that use.
