---
title: "Phase III study of tolugepant in chronic migraine prevention does not meet its primary endpoint"
date: "2026-03-12"
published: true
category: "Clinical"
summary: "The acute indication for Migrestil is not affected. The prevention programme is discontinued."
---

NeuroStat today reported that a Phase III study of tolugepant for the prevention of chronic migraine did not meet its primary endpoint of reduction in monthly migraine days versus placebo. No new safety signals were identified. The programme is discontinued. Migrestil remains approved for the acute treatment of migraine, and the study results do not affect that indication.
