title: "NeuroStat starts a Phase I study of NS-529 for postoperative delirium"
date: "2027-11-16"
published: true
category: "Clinical"
summary: "The first participants have been dosed in a single-ascending-dose study in healthy older adults."

NeuroStat today announced that the first participants have been dosed in a Phase I study of NS-529 in healthy older adults. The study will assess safety, tolerability and pharmacokinetics.
