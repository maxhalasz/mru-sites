title: "NeuroStat launches Diadem, a wearable MEG system"
date: "2028-03-14"
published: true
category: "Products"
summary: "A helmet with 64 magnetometers records brain activity while the person walks, speaks or lies down."
---

NeuroStat today launched Diadem, a helmet that records brain activity while a person moves. It holds 64 optically pumped magnetometers that work at room temperature. Diadem carries the CE mark as a class IIa device.

The first deliveries are to research hospitals in London and Boston.
