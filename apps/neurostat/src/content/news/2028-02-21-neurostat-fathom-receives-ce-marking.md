NeuroStat Diagnostics AG has received the CE mark for Fathom, a whole-body imaging system. The patient lies still in a tank of body-temperature, oxygen-carrying fluid while a ring of transducers sends sound waves through the body. Fathom turns the echoes into three-dimensional maps that compare with a whole-body MRI and adds blood flow, bone density and body composition from the same recording.

A session takes about 26 minutes. The first systems go to two university hospitals in Switzerland and Germany in the spring.
