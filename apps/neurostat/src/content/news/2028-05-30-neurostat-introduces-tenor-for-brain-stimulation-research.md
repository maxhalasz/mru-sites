---
title: "NeuroStat introduces Tenor for brain stimulation research"
date: "2028-05-30"
published: true
category: "Science"
summary: "The focused ultrasound system reaches a small region deep in the brain and works inside an MRI scanner."
---

NeuroStat has introduced Tenor, a low-intensity focused ultrasound system for research on the brain. Tenor aims a beam a few millimetres wide through the skull and works inside a 3 tesla MRI scanner, so researchers can see the target while they stimulate it.

Tenor is for research use only. Three academic centres have started studies of attention.
