The Annual General Meeting of NeuroStat Holding AG was held today in Zürich. Shareholders approved the annual report and the financial statements for 2027, a dividend of CHF 1.10 per share, and the re-election of all members of the Board of Directors. Thomas Eggenberger was re-elected as Chair.
