The Annual General Meeting of NeuroStat Holding AG took place today in Zürich. Shareholders approved the annual report and financial statements for 2027, a dividend of CHF 1.10 per share and the re-election of all members of the Board of Directors. Thomas Eggenberger was re-elected as Chair.
