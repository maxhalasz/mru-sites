category: "Products"
summary: "The 7 tesla scanner holds about 14 litres of sealed liquid helium and fits in an ordinary scanner room."
---

NeuroStat Diagnostics AG has received the CE mark for Fathom, a head-only MRI scanner with a 7 tesla magnet. The magnet is immersed in about 14 litres of sealed liquid helium, so Fathom does not need a quench pipe and can stand in a standard scanner room.

The first systems go to university hospitals in Switzerland and Germany in the second half of 2027.
