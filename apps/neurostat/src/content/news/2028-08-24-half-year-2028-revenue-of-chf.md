NeuroStat Holding AG today published results for the first half of 2028.

Revenue was CHF 603 million, up 4 percent at constant exchange rates. Core operating profit rose 6 percent to CHF 171 million. Lunavel grew 9 percent, and Baseline and Halo together grew 14 percent. Vesperil sales fell after generic ormelteon reached several markets.

The full report and presentation are in the Investors section.
