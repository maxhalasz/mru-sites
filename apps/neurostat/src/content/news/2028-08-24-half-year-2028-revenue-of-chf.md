NeuroStat Holding AG today published its results for the first half of 2028.

Revenue was CHF 603 million, an increase of 4 percent at constant exchange rates. Core operating profit rose 6 percent to CHF 171 million. Lunavel grew 9 percent, and the NeuroStat Baseline and Halo platforms grew 14 percent. Sales of Vesperil declined following the introduction of generic ormelteon in several markets.
