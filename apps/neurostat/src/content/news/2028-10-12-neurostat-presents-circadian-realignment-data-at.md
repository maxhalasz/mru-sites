---
title: "NeuroStat presents circadian realignment data at an international sleep research congress"
date: "2028-10-12"
published: true
category: "Science"
summary: "In healthy volunteers put through a simulated eight-hour shift, NS-455 advanced the melatonin rhythm, and more so at higher doses."
---

NeuroStat today presented results from a Phase I/IIa study of NS-455 in healthy adults at an international sleep research congress.

Volunteers had their sleep-wake schedule moved by eight hours. Those given NS-455 shifted their dim-light melatonin onset faster than those given placebo, and the effect grew with the dose. NS-455 was generally well tolerated.

"These data support a larger Phase II study in shift workers," said Dr. Felix Haldane, Chief Scientific Officer.
