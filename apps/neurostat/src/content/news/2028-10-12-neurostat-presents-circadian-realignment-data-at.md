---
title: "NeuroStat presents circadian realignment data at an international sleep research congress"
date: "2028-10-12"
published: true
category: "Science"
summary: "New data on NS-455 show a dose-dependent advance of the melatonin rhythm in healthy volunteers undergoing a simulated eight-hour phase shift."
---

NeuroStat today presented results from a Phase I/IIa study of NS-455 in healthy adult volunteers at an international sleep research congress.

Volunteers underwent a simulated eight-hour shift in their sleep-wake schedule. Those who received NS-455 showed a faster advance of the dim-light melatonin onset than those who received placebo, and the effect increased with dose. NS-455 was generally well tolerated.

"These data support moving NS-455 into a larger Phase II study in shift workers," said Dr. Felix Haldane, Chief Scientific Officer.
