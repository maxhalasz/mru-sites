---
title: "NeuroStat Halo adds fatigue-risk reporting for occupational health teams"
date: "2028-06-07"
published: true
category: "Products"
summary: "A software update gives occupational physicians a weekly summary of sleep and fatigue trends across a working crew."
---

NeuroStat today released an update to the Halo clinician dashboard. Occupational health teams can now see a weekly summary of sleep duration, sleep stage distribution and fatigue estimates across a crew, with individual data shown only to the supervising clinician.

The update follows field validation in offshore and shift-working populations.
