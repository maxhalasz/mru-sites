---
title: "NeuroStat Halo adds fatigue-risk reporting for occupational health teams"
date: "2028-06-07"
published: true
category: "Products"
summary: "Occupational physicians now get a weekly summary of sleep and fatigue trends for a whole crew."
---

NeuroStat today released an update to the Halo clinician dashboard. Occupational health teams can see a weekly summary of sleep duration, sleep stages and fatigue estimates for a crew. Individual data are visible only to the supervising clinician.

The update follows field validation in offshore and shift-working populations.
