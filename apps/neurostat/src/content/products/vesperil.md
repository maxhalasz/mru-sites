They raise ormelteon levels many times over.","Not recommended in severe liver impairment, and to be used with caution in moderate impairment.","Changes in prolactin and testosterone have been reported. Consider testing if a patient has absent periods, milk production, reduced libido or fertility problems.","Patients should not drive or use machinery until they know how Vesperil affects them."]
clinical: ["Approval rested on randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled studies in adults working rotating or night shifts.","The primary endpoint was total sleep time in the daytime sleep period, measured by polysomnography and sleep diary.","Compared with placebo, Vesperil increased total sleep time and shortened the time to fall asleep, and the effect held through the six-month extension.","Generic ormelteon reached several markets in 2026."]
