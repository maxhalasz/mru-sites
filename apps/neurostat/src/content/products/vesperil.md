tagline: "A sleep-timing medicine for people whose working day does not follow the sun."
how: "Ormelteon binds with high affinity to the MT1 and MT2 melatonin receptors in the suprachiasmatic nucleus, the brain's master clock. MT1 activity is associated with the onset of sleep and MT2 activity with shifting the phase of the circadian rhythm. Ormelteon has no measurable affinity for GABA receptors, which is why it is not a sedative-hypnotic in the usual sense."
safety: ["Common adverse reactions: somnolence, dizziness, fatigue and nausea.","Do not use with fluvoxamine or other strong inhibitors of CYP1A2, which raise ormelteon exposure many-fold.","Not recommended in severe hepatic impairment. Use with caution in moderate impairment.","Reports of changes in prolactin and testosterone levels: consider if amenorrhoea, galactorrhoea, reduced libido or fertility problems occur.","Patients should not drive or operate machinery until they know how Vesperil affects them."]
clinical: ["Approval was based on randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled studies in adults working rotating or night shifts.","The primary endpoint was total sleep time during the daytime sleep period, measured by polysomnography and sleep diary.","Vesperil increased total sleep time and shortened sleep latency versus placebo. Treatment effects were maintained over the six-month extension.","Generic ormelteon became available in several markets in 2026."]
