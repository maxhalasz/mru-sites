name: "Migrestil"
generic: "tolugepant"
drugClass: "Oral calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) receptor antagonist"
indication: "Acute treatment of migraine with or without aura in adults"
approved: 2020
kind: "Medicine"
published: true
tagline: "Treats a migraine attack without narrowing blood vessels."
how: "During a migraine attack, calcitonin gene-related peptide is released from trigeminal nerve endings. It adds to pain signalling and widens blood vessels in the head. Tolugepant blocks the CGRP receptor and so interrupts that signal. Unlike triptans it does not act on serotonin 5-HT1B/1D receptors and does not constrict blood vessels."
safety: ["Common side effects: nausea, sleepiness and dry mouth.","Hypersensitivity reactions such as rash and shortness of breath can occur, sometimes after a delay. Stop treatment if they do.","High blood pressure and Raynaud's phenomenon have been reported in this drug class, so blood pressure should be monitored.","Avoid strong CYP3A4 inhibitors and inducers. Use with caution in severe kidney or liver impairment.","Migrestil is approved only for acute treatment, not for preventing migraine."]
clinical: ["Approval rested on two Phase III studies in adults with two to eight moderate or severe attacks a month.","The co-primary endpoints were freedom from pain and freedom from the most bothersome symptom two hours after the dose.","In 2026 a Phase III study of tolugepant in preventing chronic migraine missed its primary endpoint, and the programme was stopped. The acute indication is not affected."]
order: 3
