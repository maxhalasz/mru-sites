indication: "Acute treatment of migraine with or without aura in adults"

kind: "Medicine"

tagline: "Acute migraine treatment without vasoconstriction."
how: "Calcitonin gene-related peptide is released from trigeminal nerve endings during a migraine attack and contributes to pain signalling and vasodilation of cranial vessels. Tolugepant blocks the CGRP receptor and so interrupts this signalling. Unlike triptans it does not act on serotonin 5-HT1B/1D receptors and does not constrict blood vessels."
safety: ["Common adverse reactions: nausea, somnolence and dry mouth.","Hypersensitivity reactions, including rash and dyspnoea, can occur and may be delayed. Discontinue if they occur.","Hypertension and Raynaud's phenomenon have been reported in the CGRP-antagonist class. Monitor blood pressure.","Avoid with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors and inducers. Use with caution in severe renal or hepatic impairment.","Migrestil is approved for acute treatment only. It is not indicated for the prevention of migraine."]
clinical: ["Approval was based on two Phase III studies in adults with a history of two to eight moderate or severe attacks per month.","The co-primary endpoints were freedom from pain and freedom from the most bothersome symptom at two hours after dose.","In 2026 a Phase III study of tolugepant for the prevention of chronic migraine did not meet its primary endpoint, and the programme was discontinued. The acute indication is not affected."]
