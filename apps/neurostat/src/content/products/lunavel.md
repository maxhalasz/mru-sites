tagline: "Lowers the signal that keeps you awake."
how: "Orexin neuropeptides keep us awake by acting on the OX1 and OX2 receptors. Corexant blocks both, which lowers the drive to stay awake and lets sleep come naturally. This is a different mechanism from benzodiazepines and other GABA-A modulators, which depress the central nervous system more broadly."
safety: ["Common side effects: sleepiness, headache, abnormal dreams and dry mouth.","Next-day impairment can occur, more often at higher doses. Patients should not drive or do anything that needs full alertness unless they are completely awake.","Complex sleep behaviours, sleep paralysis and hallucinations on falling asleep or waking have been reported.","Contraindicated in narcolepsy. Depression and suicidal thoughts can get worse, so patients should be monitored.","Avoid strong CYP3A inhibitors, and lower the dose with moderate ones."]
clinical: ["Approval was based on two Phase III studies of three months each, randomised, double-blind and placebo-controlled, plus a one-year safety study.","The co-primary endpoints were self-reported time to fall asleep and total sleep time. Wake after sleep onset was a key secondary endpoint.","The programme found no rebound insomnia or withdrawal symptoms when treatment stopped abruptly.","Lunavel is the company's largest product by revenue."]
