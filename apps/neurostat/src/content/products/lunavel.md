tagline: "Quiets the wake signal instead of forcing sleep."
how: "Orexin neuropeptides promote wakefulness by acting on the OX1 and OX2 receptors. Corexant blocks both receptors and so reduces the wake drive, allowing sleep to occur naturally. Its mechanism differs from GABA-A modulators and benzodiazepines, which broadly depress central nervous system activity."
safety: ["Common adverse reactions: somnolence, headache, abnormal dreams and dry mouth.","Next-day impairment: patients should not drive or perform activities requiring full alertness unless they are fully awake. Risk is higher at higher doses.","Complex sleep behaviours, sleep paralysis and hypnagogic or hypnopompic hallucinations have been reported.","Contraindicated in narcolepsy. Worsening of depression and suicidal ideation can occur: monitor patients.","Avoid with strong CYP3A inhibitors. Reduce the dose with moderate CYP3A inhibitors."]
clinical: ["Two Phase III, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled studies of three months each supported approval, with a one-year safety study.","Co-primary endpoints were subjective time to sleep onset and subjective total sleep time. Wake after sleep onset was a key secondary endpoint.","No evidence of rebound insomnia or withdrawal symptoms was seen after abrupt discontinuation in the clinical programme.","Lunavel is the largest product by revenue in the NeuroStat portfolio."]
